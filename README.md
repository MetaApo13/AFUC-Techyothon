# AFUC-Techyothon

Incident Management and Response Website for Schools ------------------------------------------------------------

The solution is a comprehensive AI-powered web application designed to enhance school safety through real-time incident detection, response, and reporting. It integrates video analytics, sensor data, and manual reporting to identify a wide range of incidents—from bullying and trespassing to medical emergencies and fire drills. The system provides an intuitive dashboard with role-based access, live incident feeds, an interactive school map, and AI-generated summaries that help administrators, teachers, and students stay informed and take immediate action. By combining advanced detection technologies with a user-friendly interface, the platform ensures faster responses, trend monitoring, and a safer school environment while maintaining strict privacy and ethical standards.
The overarching goal is to enhance the safety and responsiveness of schools and campuses through proactive detection and coordinated response.
